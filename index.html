A heritage passed down through generations.

A story told through taste.

Fekkas is one of Morocco’s most cherished biscuits, shared at the tea table for generations. Fekass & More imagines each selection as a journey through the flavors and memories of Morocco.

A savoir-faire passed down to us, reinterpreted today with precision and creativity, to make Fekass more than just a biscuit: a Moroccan signature. Established 2020, Marrakech.
